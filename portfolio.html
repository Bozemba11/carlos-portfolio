The problem isn't that people don't know how to fix IT issues — it's that finding the right fix takes longer than the fix itself. This tool flips that. Describe your problem in plain English ("my Linux VM won't start after a kernel update") and get back a structured diagnosis: root cause, severity level, step-by-step fix, tools needed, and how to prevent it next time. Who I Am

The Rare Overlap

There's a gap in most teams. The developers understand the system but not the business. The strategists understand the goal but can't build toward it. I trained on both sides deliberately — because the most valuable problems live exactly in that gap.

On the networking side, I've configured Cisco switches, built MikroTik router setups from the ground up, designed VLAN architectures, deployed DHCP/DNS infrastructure, and secured networks with firewall rules and VPNs. I've also traced kernel panics at 2am, debugged Odoo database corruption, and researched why a product wasn't converting. That range isn't accidental — it's the whole point. AI is now the third layer, letting me automate the repetitive parts of all three worlds and move faster on what actually matters.

I don't build impressive-looking things that don't work. I build boring, reliable tools that solve real problems — and I document them well enough that someone else can maintain them.
